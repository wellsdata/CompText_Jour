Headnote Abstract - Social media platforms are constantly faced with rapid news which are disseminated based on relative importance, tone, and intended audience. Fake news is made up of stories written to deceive the readers of these social media platforms. Stance Detection is one of the factors influencing fake news detection. It is not practical for humans to fact-check every piece of information produced by the media. Hence, the goal of this research is to use natural language processing techniques to automate stance detection to determine the quality of the news source. It considers what other organizations write about the same headline. A body of the text is claimed to agree, disagree, discuss, or be unrelated to a headline. The system was developed by preprocessing the text according to the language grammar and, mixing convolutional neural network and recurrent neural network (RNN). Therefore, it performs with an accuracy of 67.1% and, an error result of 32.9%.
Keywords: Stance, News Dataset, Afaan Oromo, Detection.
I.INTRODUCTION
News is an account of what is happening around us. It may involve current events, propaganda, new initiatives, or ongoing projects, or other issues [1-3]. News writing structure or style is how elements of the news are presented based on relative importance, tone, and intended audience [4], [5]. Besides, it is also concerned with the structure of vocabulary and sentences [2], [3].
Fake news is defined as a "made-up story to deceive, geared towards getting clicks". Stance Detection is one of the factors influencing fake news detection. It is not practical for humans to fact-check every piece of information produced by the media. Hence, the goal of this project is to use natural language processing techniques to automate stance detection to determine the quality of the news source. It considers what other organizations write about the same headline. A body of the text is claimed to agree, disagree, discuss, or be unrelated to a headline.
Afaan Oromo is a Cushitic language spoken by about 50 million people in Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia, and Egypt, and it is the third-largest language in Africa. The Oromo people are the largest ethnic group in Ethiopia and account for more than 40% of the population.
The Oromo language, also known as Afaan Oromo by using this language peoples communicate through social media. Since social media is used as a means of communication that can disseminate information quickly and widely [6]. In recent years, the exponential growth of social media such as Facebook, Twitter, linked line has been increasingly exploited for the propagation of hate speech and the organization of hate-based activities [7] as there are many positive impacts of social media are there, there are also many negative impacts like hate speech which is attacking peoples based on characteristics like race, ethnicity, gender, and sexual orientation, or threats of violence towards others.
But there is no formal definition that exists but there is a consensus that it is speech that targets disadvantaged social groups in a manner that is potentially harmful to them [8], [9]. Drawing upon these definitions, we define hate speech-language that is used to express hatred towards a targeted group or is intended to be derogatory to the members of the group. In extreme cases, this may also be language that threatens or incites violence, but it does not include all instances of offensive language because people often use terms that are highly offensive to certain groups but in a qualitatively different manner. Fake news is an unfortunately common occurrence on social media and in some cases culminates in severe threats to individuals' groups of people by verbal attacks and promotion of hatred based on race, ethnicity, and national origin [9].
Hate speech is prevalent and challenging in the Ethiopian online community as individuals spread hate speech hiding behind their screens. Possibly leads to acts of violence or hate crimes and destroys the lives of individuals, families, communities, and the country at large.
Afaan Oromo is one of the sub-Saharan countries' natural Languages which is written in Latin script. II.RELATED WORKS
The paper [9] explores the application of natural language processing techniques with multinomial naive Bayes for the detection of "fake news" on 752 news datasets that were prepared in collaboration with linguistic and journalism experts of the Afaan Oromo language. According to [9] the above data set was tested on multinomial naive Bayes classification algorithm with Term frequency, and term frequency-inverse document frequency (TF-IDF) of unigram and bi-grams and find that term frequency of unigram of this model identifies fake news sources with an accuracy of 96%, having slight effects on recall.
The paper [10], conducted on Afaan Oromo fake news detection system; involves preprocessing like tokenization, normalization, stop word removing and abbreviation resolving, feature extraction-like TermFrequency-inverted document frequency, term frequency, and hash to know word importance that appears in the news and word appears in the corpus and N-grams which are a powerful Natural Language Processing technique to capture semantic and syntactic sequences was also used. All possible combinations of feature extraction techniques and natural processing techniques were used with a passive-aggressive classification algorithm. Passiveaggressive performs 97.2% with a classification error of 2.8% which was better than ensemble algorithms like gradient boosting and random forest and linear classifier like multinomial Naive Bayes. Finally, a python Django was used for the web-based deployment of the model system using the term frequency-inverted document frequency feature extraction with unigram and passiveaggressive classification algorithm [10, 11].
The major contribution of this paper is to detect agreement, disagreement, relation and irrelativeness of a news with a given headline from Afaan Oromo Language news on a social media to determine the truth of the news. This method is more accurate than that of binary labelled fake news detection, in detecting time line news. This is not based only on the composition of the words, it also considers the news publisher history and agreement of its news with mostly believed media authors. The model is more accurate than other models because of the hybrid combination of deep neural network like LSTM and CNN.
III.METHODOLOGY
The proposed approach includes the three important stages namely: Data Gathering, Data pre-processing, supervised training, and the current section includes brief discussions of the same.
A.DATASET GATHERING
During the study and system development, different data was collected from different sources based on their relevance to system development and study. Authors discuss how this data was prepared after the methodology.
To build a lexicon of Afaan Oromo cue phrases, synonyms, abbreviations, stop words, and names of weekdays, months, and lexicon data were collected from secondary sources like Afaan Oromo textbook, newspaper, fiction, journal articles, dictionaries, media documents, and websites for preprocessing purposes.
To train and evaluate the performance of the system it is mandatory to prepare the train and test data corpus respectively. Therefore, to train the model and test the performance of the system like training data and testing data are also gathered from the social media; mostly from Facebook accounts and pages as sources, accordingly. The reason why these sources are selected is that they are the most widely used social media in Ethiopia, Especially Facebook. Because 80% of troll or hoax information on Facebook is from users usually interacting with the content already [12].
News data sets were collected manually because the accounts and pages can post the news in different languages, and they were collected from sources that have relatively large amounts of followers and the most common news sources on Facebook because they can easily mislead or misinform the massive society.
The time limits the fake or reality lives long, so as time elapse it verify reality so the collected data was the pieces of news that are already verified. On the same title, both fake and real news was collected and labeled carefully by the integration of linguistic and journalism experts to clearly show the best distinctions between fake and real. This enables the model to easily learn the characteristics of fakers and real sources.
B.DA TA PREPROCESSING COMPONENT
Data preprocessing is done to convert the raw data into a required format. In this research, the datasets are collected from different resources that have different formats and attributes manually. Hence, the data can be duplicated and they may contain some attributes which are not useful. So, it is a must to convert the data into the required format with the required attributes that are used to train the model. To do so different duplications and unnecessary tokens like punctuation, URL, and HTML should be removed.
The Preprocessing component takes Afaan Oromo news from the search engine component and news datasets and undergoes some preprocessing steps to convert the news into a form that eases the detection process. This component includes tokenize, stop words removal, and abbreviation resolution. So, this process cleans the text; changing words to lowercase and removing punctuation, links, and abnormal characters. After that we will tokenize, the texts using spaCy's package, removing stop words, as these words are likely to just create noise rather than provide any true meaning.
