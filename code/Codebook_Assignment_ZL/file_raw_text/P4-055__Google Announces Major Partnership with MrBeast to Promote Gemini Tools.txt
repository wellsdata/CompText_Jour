September 2nd, 2026 ( Android Headlines  - Delivered by  Newstex )
Google just announced a brand new multi-year partnership with Jimmy Donaldson, widely known as MrBeast, to promote its AI platforms, including Gemini. MrBeast, the first creator to hit 500 million subscribers on YouTube, will use Gemini to help plan and execute his stunt videos. He'll also highlight how Google Health and the new Fitbit Air can easily track his fitness goals in these videos.
MrBeast will use AI to survive extreme climate challenges
On September 5th, MrBeast will be releasing a video. In the video, the YouTuber and his crew will face harsh climate conditions across the jungle, desert, and the Arctic. During the race to survive these extreme environments, the team will utilize Gemini to figure out potential dangers and adapt to sudden weather changes. The AI tool helps them map travel paths and manage useful gear supplies during the trip.
The whole concept behind the video is how people can use Gemini as a real-time partner to manage complicated logistics and solve any problems on the go. Instead of spending hours going through survival guides, people can ask quick questions to the AI to solve problems as they happen.
The partnership also focuses on tracking daily wellness with fitness tools
Google's partnership with MrBeast for Gemini will also heavily feature the fitness tracking products from the search titan. In future video content, MrBeast will integrate the new Fitbit Air into his physical challenges. He'll use the videos to show viewers how they can use the health tracking devices to support their daily workout routines and manage long-term wellness targets.
If MrBeast's early content performs well, we can expect Google to feature MrBeast in a broader campaign to demonstrate the practical benefits of Gemini and the Google Health app. We'll have more details in the coming weeks, so stay tuned.
