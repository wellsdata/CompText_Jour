Body


Electronic health records (EHRs) are one of the most critical parts of a health system's operations. Since they store patient records, EHRs are treasure troves of data and insights that are becoming increasingly valuable with the development of new AI tools.
EHRs are also core to the provider and employee experience. They have arguably increased the reporting burden for doctors and nurses since their implementation. Health system leaders and EHR developers are tasked with making these necessary systems as user-friendly as possible so that clinicians can focus on patient care.
Epic Systems is currently the leading EHR vendor with a market share of more than 37 percent, according to Definitive Healthcare.
From August 18 through August 21, 2025, Epic is hosting health care executives, clinicians, IT professionals, partners and other industry stakeholders at its annual User Group Meeting (UGM).
Members of the media were invited to Epic's sprawling headquarters in Verona, Wisconsin, on August 19.
Follow Newsweek's live blog for updates throughout the day.
Epic is expected to make multiple major AI announcements today, including its own ambient scribing tool and further automation of its data analytics and exploration product, SlicerDicer.
We'll start the day at 9:30 a.m. EST with an executive address from Judy Faulkner, CEO and founder of Epic Systems.
During lunch, we'll hear from Sean Bina (VP of access and patient experience), Jackie Gerhart (chief medical officer) and Ryan Krause (VP of revenue cycle operations).
In the afternoon, several customers will share their experiences using Epic products for specific use cases.
Members of the media are prohibited from publishing photos, but Epic has promised to share some pictures from the day. I'll update this blog once I receive them.
