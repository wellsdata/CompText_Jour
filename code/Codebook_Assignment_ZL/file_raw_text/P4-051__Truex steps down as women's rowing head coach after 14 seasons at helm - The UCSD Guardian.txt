Truex steps down as women's rowing head coach after 14 seasons at helm
Alan Zhang, Sports Co-Editor
·
Jun 30, 2026
On June 16, UC San Diego Athletics announced that women's rowing head coach Colin Truex has left the shoreline. Truex took over ahead of the 2013 season after serving as an assistant coach for the men's rowing program for six years.
"After careful thought and consideration and consultations with loved ones, I've decided to step down as head coach of the UC San Diego women's rowing program," Truex said in a press release. "It has been a great pleasure and honor to serve in the role for over a decade, but it is time for me to move on to the next chapter of my life, whatever that may be. I've absolutely loved working with this team and we had many great years together."
Truex inked a two-year, $188k extension in 2025. His contract was due to expire in June 2027, according to contracts obtained by The UCSD Guardian through a public records request. UCSD associate head coach Kim Dale will serve as the interim as Athletics searches for a new head coach.
Taking over during UCSD's Division-II days, Truex led the team to four NCAA Division II Championship appearances. In the 2018 tournament, the Tritons finished second in the Varsity Eight Grand Final, six seconds behind Central Oklahoma. Since transitioning to Division I in 2020, UCSD saw the most success in 2024. The Tritons finished second at the CAA Championships and Truex was named CAA Coach of the Year. Truex's departure comes a month after the 2026 season's conclusion, when UCSD placed fifth at the CAA Championships.
