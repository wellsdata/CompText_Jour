Searching the web usually requires you to stop what you are doing, open a tab, and actively look for updates. Well, a major tech giant wants to change that dynamic entirely. Following its initial tease at I/O 2026, Google has officially started rolling out 'Search agents' in its AI Mode. The upgrade turns the traditional search engine into a proactive assistant that works quietly in the background.
The first wave of this rollout introduces 'information agents' that can monitor the internet so you don't have to.
How the background trackers work
Instead of waiting for you to type a query, these new information agents run 24/7. They constantly scan blogs, news platforms, social media, and real-time databases covering everything from financial markets to shopping inventory and sports statistics. The goal is to catch relevant changes the moment they happen.
Setting one up is relatively straightforward. In AI Mode, users can simply type phrases like 'keep me updated on' or 'alert me when' followed by their specific criteria. For instance, they could instruct the agent to track local real estate listings that match your exact budget and square footage requirements. They could also ask it to monitor when a specific athlete drops a new sneaker collaboration. Once the agent finds a match, it delivers a synthesized update and offers quick actions to follow up (via 9to5Google).
The speed advantage over regular scheduled tasks
Google already offers automated tools, such as scheduled actions in the standard Gemini app or frequent checks via Gemini Spark. However, those features generally rely on set intervals, running once a day or every 15 minutes.
The main selling point for these new Search agents is immediate delivery. As they reason across web data continuously, they can flag crucial information much faster than a standard timer-based app. This gives users a slight edge when tracking fast-moving news or limited-stock retail drops.
Availability and pricing
As with many of Google's advanced automated tools, this feature is launching as a premium experience. Information agents are currently rolling out exclusively to Google AI Ultra subscribers, a top-tier service that sits at $99.99 or $199.99 per month. The feature supports all languages and markets currently active within AI Mode.
If you are not on the highest tier, you will not have to wait indefinitely. Google plans to bring these autonomous Search agents to the more affordable Google AI Pro tier later this summer. For now, early adopters can start offloading their daily internet stalking habits to the cloud.
