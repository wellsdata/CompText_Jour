1 Meta pauses teen access to AI characters: Meta is halting teens’ access to artificial intelligence characters, at least temporarily, the company said in a blog post Friday. Meta Platforms Inc., which owns Instagram and WhatsApp, said that starting in the coming weeks, teens will no longer be able to access AI characters “until the updated experience is ready.” This applies to anyone who gave Meta a birthday that makes them a minor, as well as people who claim to be adults who Meta suspects are not based on its age prediction technology.
2 Review confirms Hunter S. Thompson’s death was a suicide: Investigators in Colorado say they have confirmed that the 2005 shooting death of journalist Hunter S. Thompson was a suicide. That follows a review requested by local authorities at the behest of Thompson’s widow. The Colorado Bureau of Investigation says the probe was opened after Anita Thompson contacted the sheriff for the Aspen area with “new concerns and potential information regarding the investigation.” The agency has not explained what that information was. But it says the review found that Thompson’s body “was not moved or ‘staged’ after death.” Anita Thompson said the review allows everyone who loved her husband “to move forward with a clean conscience.”
3 Suicide bombing kills 7: Police say a suicide bomber detonated an explosive vest among guests at a wedding ceremony in Pakistan, killing at least seven people and wounding 25. The attack on Friday took place at the home of Noor Alam Mehsud, a pro-government elder. No group immediately claimed responsibility.
But suspicion probably will fall on the Pakistani Taliban, also known as Tehrike-Taliban Pakistan or TTP, a group that has carried out numerous attacks in recent years. The group is separate from but allied with the Afghan Taliban.
4 Recall for Toyota trucks: Toyota announced Friday it would recall about 162,000 pickup trucks in the U.S. after it was discovered that the vehicles’ multimedia displays could compromise driver safety. The recall affects the company’s model year 2024-2025 Tundra and Tundra Hybrid line of trucks. Affected customers are being notified, according to a statement from Toyota.
5 Wisconsin lawmakers meeting outside of public view: The shutdown of Wisconsin’s version of C-SPAN amid a shortfall of fundraising has left the state as perhaps the only one in the country where its legislative floor debates are not being broadcast. A bipartisan proposal to bring the network back was introduced on Thursday, but it is unclear if it has enough support to pass.
